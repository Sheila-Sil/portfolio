About me.

I'm Sheila, a student at Seven Lakes High School in Katy, Texas. I write software and teach younger kids to write it too.

Most of what I make starts with a question: how would this feel to use? LaunchOS came from wanting a calmer way to plan a project. ByteBridge came from watching friends get stuck on the same bug I once had. When I'm not coding I'm playing bass, producing R&B, skateboarding, or telling a joke that is almost certainly too cheesy.
